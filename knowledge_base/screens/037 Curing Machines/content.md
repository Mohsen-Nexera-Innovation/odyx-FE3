### Section 2 · Why washing & curing matters
- **Header:** A printed part isn't a finished part
- **Body:** Off the printer, every surface still carries a film of uncured resin, and the
  material underneath has only partly polymerized. First the part is washed in isopropyl
  alcohol to strip that film from fine details and deep cavities. Then post-curing under UV
  light completes the reaction the printer started — hardening the part to its full mechanical
  strength. Skip either step and you seat a restoration that is weaker, less accurate, and
  still carrying uncured resin. The ODYX Cure UV-02 is built for that second step: the cure
  that makes the part clinical.
- **Mini-timeline microcopy:** PRINT — the part takes shape, layer by layer → WASH — an IPA
  bath strips uncured resin → CURE — the UV-02 completes polymerization.
- *(No description line under the header beyond the body itself — review #20.)*

### Section 3 · Washed. Then cured. *(dark chamber sequence)*
- **Act I — WASHED:** Fresh from the printer, the part goes through its alcohol wash — every
  fine detail stripped of uncured resin.
- **Act II — CURED:** Inside the UV-02, light reaches every surface — 360° coverage, wavelengths
  matched to the resin, until the part is fully hardened.
- **Closing line:** One to five minutes, typically. Then it's ready for the patient.

### Section 4 · Feature chips *(five, client's row — values from p14)*

### Take / reject

**Take**
- **Formlabs' "Why Post-Curing Matters" educational block** — explains cross-linking and ties it to clinical outcomes. The client's own proposed content starts with "Why curing matters"; Formlabs proves the pattern works.
- **Formlabs' Print → Wash → Cure timeline** — our workflow spine in miniature, and the honest place to acknowledge washing now that no wash product is on the site. Numbers from catalog only.
- **NanoCure's per-appliance cure-time cards** — indication + minutes beats prose. Ours ships catalog-sourced: the p15 table is the ODYX Cure's own.
- **NanoCure's wavelength-science framing** — theirs is 365+385; ours is 365/385/405 *selectable*, which is a stronger one-box story. Frame, don't copy: every number from p14.
- **Mechanism-led feature blocks** (all SprintRay pages) — say *why*, not just "powerful".
- **Datasheet offload** — full spec table on page (the client drew one), PDF as secondary CTA per the client's "Downloads" inner link.
- **Vocabulary** for SEO §3: "post-curing", "curing unit", "wash and cure", "UV curing", "validated settings", "biocompatibility", "mechanical properties".

**Reject**
- **Superlatives without evidence** ("most advanced on the planet") — claims stay catalog-cited.
- **Cloud/smart features** — the UV-02 has presets, not a cloud; the honest story is 8 profiles.
- **On-page prices** (Formlabs) — pricing unresolved (products.md Q7).
- **Instant-heat / thermodynamics story** (ProCure 2, NanoCure) — heating is exactly our unsourced claim (§7 ⚠); nothing ships until the client confirms.
- **Stratasys' "no post-processing needed" frame** — competes against the product's existence; expect the objection from PolyJet-aware labs, answer with LCD/DLP economics.
- **Buy-now hero CTA** — "Request a Demo" is the ODYX conversion path (client reference shows it).

### Where we beat all four

SprintRay splits post-processing across three pages and three devices; Formlabs across two units
and a bundle. None of the four places the cure step inside a five-step patient-to-delivery
narrative — their ecosystem strips are footers, not spines. This page is the WASH & CURE chapter
of the ODYX story, anchored by one precision cure box whose triple-wavelength selection covers
the entire resin range — a per-application cure story every dentist can act on (p15).
